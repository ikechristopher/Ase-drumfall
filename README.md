ÀṢẸ: DRUMFALL

Every blow is a word. Every fight is a song. Do not let the world go silent.

A Yoruba-inspired mythic action game concept.

About the Project

ÀṢẸ: DRUMFALL is a proposed action game inspired by Yoruba mythology, cosmology, rhythm, and storytelling.

When the Great Drum, Ìlù Àṣẹ, falls silent, the connection between Ayé (Earth) and Òrun (the spiritual realm) is threatened. Ògún must confront a growing force known as Ìdákẹ́ — the Silence — and fight to restore the world's rhythm.

The Creative Vision

The project explores how rhythm, mythology, combat, and player choice can work together to create a distinctive game experience rooted in Yoruba-inspired world-building.

Core Design Pillars

- Rhythm-based combat: A combat concept built around three tones, call-and-response encounters, and expressive combinations.
- Mythic world-building: A connected world shaped by the Iron Road, the Great Drum, and the relationship between Ayé and Òrun.
- Character-driven storytelling: A mythic cast including Ògún, Ṣàngó, Ọ̀ṣun, Ọya, and Èṣù.
- Meaningful choices: A proposed Mend-or-Slay path that influences character development and possible endings.
- Cultural integrity: A commitment to research, Yoruba cultural consultation, native-speaker language review, and respectful representation of living traditions.
